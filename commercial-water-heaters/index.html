Warning Signs to Watch For

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you money and keep a slow drip from turning into a flooded utility room. Keep an eye out for the following:

  • Unsteady delivery temperatures that suggest a failing mixing valve
  • Distant fixtures that take minutes to run warm
  • Rumbling, popping, or banging from a heavily used tank
  • Hot water running out during your busiest hour
  • Rust-colored water, leaks at fittings, or moisture under the heater
  • An aging unit you would rather replace on your schedule than in an emergency
  • Rising gas or electric bills with no change in business volume
  • Water temperatures that worry you before a health inspection

Prevention Tips That Actually Help

A few simple habits, like an annual flush and a quick look at the relief valve, can add years to a tank. Here are our recommendations:

  • Flush high-demand heaters on a regular interval to keep recovery strong
  • Keep the area around the heaters clear for combustion air and service access
  • Plan replacement before an aging unit fails during peak season
  • Record fixture temperatures regularly, especially before inspections
  • Verify delivery temperatures at sinks and showers a few times a year
  • Listen for a failing recirculation pump and have check valves inspected
  • Inspect anode rods regularly, since heavy use consumes them faster
