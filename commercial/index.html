Safety

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in tanks stored too cool, and expensive water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.

After-Hours Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A commercial water heater failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens can't sanitize, salons turn clients away, hotel guests complain, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling fast for emergency repairs anywhere in Beaverton and the surrounding area, and planned work can be booked after hours to limit downtime.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including failed gas valves, burned-out heating elements, flame sensor and ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks and fittings, recirculation pumps that stopped, and tankless error-code lockouts. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records.
