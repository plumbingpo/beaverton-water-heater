Keeping Your Tigard Water Heater Running Longer

Most premature tank failures start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Flush the tank every year to clear sediment, have the anode rod inspected every few years, and test the relief valve each year so it doesn't seize. Keep the temperature near 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. If you have a tankless unit, descale it yearly where the water is hard to protect the heat exchanger.

How old is your water heater? The rating plate on the tank usually includes the month and year it was built, and its age says a lot about what comes next. Most tank models make it to 8 to 12 years, heat pump units 10 to 15, and well-maintained tankless units around 20. If your heater is beyond that range and is also showing rusty water, popping sounds, or moisture at the base, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install once the tank fails.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but once it's gone, the water starts eating the tank steel, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a flooded utility room.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Tigard

Whatever type of water heater your Tigard home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
